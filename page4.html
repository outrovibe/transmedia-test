MEG'S NOTES

What I found

I think I finally understand what Dad was trying to show me.

Mzanzi Connect is not just a social media platform. IT controls what people can find on it. They decide which stories are promoted, which information is difficult to find and which things disappear.

They remove languages, songs, stories and photographs. They make some parts of our history harder to see until people stop looking for them.

And Charles is inside it too.

They have made him believe that the information they give him is the only information that matters.

Dad found out what they were doing. He tried to expose them, but IT found him first.

They captured him and forced him to help maintain the system. They used his scientific knowledge to make it stronger.

I can't fix everything they have done. Dad already tried. But I can try to save Charles from them.

I also need to get Dad back.

I don't know how to do that yet.

I need to ask W.
